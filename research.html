A particle becomes a cloud droplet only when the air around it is humid enough, and the threshold depends on the particle's size and composition. Get that relationship wrong and climate models get clouds wrong. My work is on the measurement end of it.

Experimental

Size-selected CCN activation

A differential mobility analyzer selects a single mobility diameter from a polydisperse aerosol stream. The resulting monodisperse flow is split in two: a condensation particle counter counts every particle present, while a CCN-100 counter counts only those that activate into droplets at a set supersaturation. The ratio of the two, scanned across diameters and supersaturations, gives the activation curve and with it the critical diameter.

Method

Correcting for chamber wall loss

Over the course of a chamber experiment, particles are continuously lost to the walls by diffusion and gravitational settling. The measured size distribution therefore decays even when no chemistry is occurring, and because the loss rate is strongly size-dependent, the distribution changes shape rather than simply decreasing in magnitude. Any quantity derived from it inherits that distortion.

I fit an eddy-diffusivity wall-loss model to the SMPS scan series and apply the correction per size bin, so that the reported distribution describes the aerosol suspended in the chamber rather than the fraction that happened to reach the inlet.

Software

Analysis infrastructure for the laboratory

Two instruments recording the same experiment rarely agree about the time, and an offset of a few seconds is enough to smear an activation curve. I lead the development of the laboratory's analysis application, which takes that over along with the rest of the routine handling:

  • Detects instrument files as they are written and identifies the run to which each belongs
  • Corrects clock offsets between instruments before any merge is attempted
  • Joins CCN records with their matching DMA and CPC records into a single analysed run
  • Maintains a searchable library of processed runs, so earlier results are reopened rather than rederived
  • Streams live readings from a LabJack on a Raspberry Pi while an experiment is in progress
